# 4Cr13 and X46CrS13

## 1. Grades
4Cr13 is a martensitic stainless steel with about 0.4 % carbon and 13 % chromium. X46CrS13 is a similar grade with added sulphur for better machinability; the sulphur forms manganese sulphide inclusions that can lower transverse ductility.

## 2. Chemistry window used in this project
Carbon 0.37 to 0.45 %, chromium 12.4 to 13.6 %, plus silicon, manganese, phosphorus, sulphur and nickel as recorded per heat. These are the ranges observed in the project data set, used as the plausibility window for incoming data.

## 3. Why carbon and chromium matter
Carbon sets the hardness of the martensite: more carbon means higher hardness and lower ductility after the same tempering. Chromium gives corrosion resistance; part of it binds with carbon as carbides, which reduces free chromium if tempering is too hot.

## 4. Part variants
Axles are produced at 8 mm diameter; a later design change moved part of the production to 7.8 mm. Some variants are die-cast into a housing (press-fit or overmoulding), which adds a thermal and mechanical load after heat treatment.
